Vazirmatn v33.003

Install Vazirmatn-Regular.ttf and Vazirmatn-Bold.ttf before opening or presenting the PowerPoint on another computer.
On Windows: select both TTF files, right-click, and choose Install.
On macOS: open the TTF files in Font Book and install.
On Linux: install with the font viewer or place them in your user font directory.

The companion PDF embeds its fonts and requires no installation.
The fonts are unmodified and distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.
Official source: https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn/tree/v33.003
